## Terms of Service — Dishari Surveys

_Last updated: Oct 3rd, 2026_

1. **What this is.** Dishari Surveys is a feedback tool run by Dishari Inc. ("Dishari", "we") to collect opinions about our events.
2. **Your link.** Your survey link is personal and works for one submission. Please don't share it. Once you submit, you can't change your answers.
3. **Be respectful.** Comments must not be abusive, hateful, threatening, or include other people's personal information. We may remove comments that break these rules.
4. **How we use your feedback.** By submitting, you allow Dishari to use your answers and comments, without your name, to improve our events. We may quote comments anonymously in internal reports.
5. **No guarantees.** The service is provided "as is". We may change or close a survey at any time.
6. **Limits to anonymity.** Your responses are anonymous in normal use: they are shown and reported under a randomly generated display name, not your name or email address. However, Dishari may look into a response if we have reasonable grounds to suspect fraud, misuse of the survey (for example, a shared or misused link), or content that is abusive, threatening, or unlawful. In those cases only, a small number of authorised Dishari admins may use the participation code attached to the response to identify which survey link it came from, and the member that link was sent to. We will do this only as far as needed to investigate and respond, keep the matter confidential, and record who carried out the review and why. We will not use this process to identify people because of opinions or criticism they have shared in good faith. Where the law requires it, we may share relevant information with the authorities.
7. **Contact.** Questions: support@dishariboston.org

## Privacy Policy — Dishari Surveys

_Last updated: Oct 3rd, 2026_

**What we collect**
- Your answers (Yes/No votes, written answers and comments).
- Your one-time participation code, so we can make sure each link is used only once.
- A randomly generated display name (for example "blacktiger12") attached to your responses.
- Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security (such as IP address), held for a short period and not linked to your answers.

**What we don't collect**
- We do not ask for your name, and we don't store your email address in the survey system. Emails were sent separately using our member list.
- We keep the list matching survey links to email addresses separately from the survey system, accessible only to the Dishari executive committee, and delete it 60 days after the survey closes. Until then, it may be used only for the review described in our Terms of Service (section 6).

**Who can see your responses**
- Only authorised Dishari admins. Other participants cannot see your answers or comments.
- Results may be shared within Dishari's committee as anonymous summaries (counts, percentages and unattributed comments).
- In the limited cases described in section 6 of our Terms of Service, an authorised admin may identify the member a response came from.

**Where data is stored**
- On Dishari's own web server, hosted by Hostinger in United States of America.

**How long we keep it**
- Responses are kept for 24 months to compare events year-on-year, then deleted.

**Your choices**
- Taking part is voluntary. To ask about or remove your response, email support@dishariboston.org with your participation code or pseudonym provided at the end of the survey. Participant code is the 8-character code at the end of the participation url.

**Contact**
